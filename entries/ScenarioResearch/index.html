In the 1980s, neoliberalism swept through Latin America like a tornado. However, its failings eventually built resentment on the populace, causing a “pink tide” to start forming. The first state to be hit by this tide was Venezuela with Hugo Chávez. In our world, Hugo Chávez fundamentally redefined Latin American history by starting a massive wave of socialists (Lula, AMLO, Kirchner, etc) to pop up in LatAm.
This is not our world. In this world, Hugo Chávez was elected but deposed in the April coup. This, combined with stronger foreign intervention by the United States, stopped the “pink wave” in its tracks. This is a story about how neoliberalism sucks, about how countries suffer from it, about how dissent is quelled, about dictators masquerading under the veneer of liberalism, and about one very Collorful man.
